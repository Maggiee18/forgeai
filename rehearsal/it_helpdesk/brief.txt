We want an internal IT helpdesk assistant for Corvane Analytics employees in Bengaluru and Pune. Most tickets we get are questions people could answer themselves: password and security rules, what to do about a lost laptop or a phishing email, how to raise a ticket and how fast we respond, and how to request a laptop, monitor or software, including what each item costs, who has to approve it and how long it takes to arrive. The assistant should answer from our IT policies and the device catalogue, be quick and practical, and send people to the Service Desk when it can't help. It should also tell employees the live status of their open tickets and whether any systems are down right now.
