# Code of Conduct and Grievance Procedure
Meridian Shipmanagement Pvt Ltd | Document HR-POL-01 | Edition 2026

1. Working Hours
Office hours are 9:30 am to 6:00 pm, Monday to Friday, with a 45 minute lunch break. Shore staff may work from home on up to 2 days per week under the hybrid work arrangement, with the agreement of the reporting manager. Staff in the marine operations team follow a separate 24 hour duty roster.

2. Attendance
Attendance is recorded through the biometric system. Arriving after 10:00 am is recorded as a late mark. Three late marks in a calendar month are treated as half a day of casual leave.

3. Dress Code
Business casual dress is expected in the office. Employees visiting a vessel, shipyard or terminal must wear safety shoes, a helmet and a high visibility vest, and must complete the site safety induction before boarding.

4. Gifts and Hospitality
Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from suppliers, agents or clients only if the value is below INR 2,000 per occasion. Any gift of INR 2,000 or more must be declared to the Compliance Officer within 5 working days and is either returned or donated. Cash gifts of any amount must be refused.

5. Conflicts of Interest
Employees must declare any personal or family financial interest in a supplier, agent or client in the annual conflict of interest declaration, submitted each April, and whenever a new conflict arises.

6. Confidentiality and Social Media
Information about vessels, cargo, clients, crew and incidents is confidential. Employees must not post photographs taken on board vessels or comment on company incidents on social media. Only the Corporate Communications team may speak to the media.

7. Prevention of Sexual Harassment
The company has an Internal Committee under the POSH Act 2013 at each office. A complaint should be made in writing to the Internal Committee within 3 months of the incident. The Internal Committee completes its inquiry within 90 days of receiving the complaint. Retaliation against anyone who complains or gives evidence is treated as serious misconduct.

8. Grievance Procedure
Employees with a work related grievance should follow these levels in order.
Level 1: raise the grievance with the reporting manager, who must respond within 5 working days.
Level 2: if unresolved, escalate in writing to the Head of Department, who must respond within 7 working days.
Level 3: if still unresolved, escalate to the Grievance Committee chaired by the Head of HR, which gives a final decision within 15 working days.
Grievances can be raised in the HRMS portal under Employee Relations. Harassment complaints go directly to the Internal Committee and skip these levels.

9. Disciplinary Process
Minor misconduct leads to a verbal warning followed by a written warning. Serious misconduct, such as fraud, theft, violence, working under the influence of alcohol, or a breach of confidentiality, leads to a show cause notice. The employee must reply to a show cause notice within 3 working days. A disciplinary inquiry may result in suspension, withholding of increment, or termination.

10. Whistleblowing
Concerns about fraud, safety violations or unethical conduct can be reported confidentially to the Ethics Officer by email at ethics@meridianship.example or through the anonymous ethics hotline. Reports are acknowledged within 2 working days. No employee will be penalised for a report made in good faith.
