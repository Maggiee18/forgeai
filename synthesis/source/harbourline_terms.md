# Supplier Commercial Terms: Harbourline Spares
Approved supplier HLS-042 | Terms valid 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026

1. Payment Terms
Invoices are payable net 30 days from the date of invoice. No early payment discount is offered.

2. Volume Discounts
A volume discount is applied per purchase order on the total order value before freight. Orders of USD 2,500 or more receive a 3 percent discount. Orders of USD 10,000 or more receive a 6 percent discount. Orders of USD 25,000 or more receive a 10 percent discount. Only the highest applicable tier applies.

3. Urgent Orders
Urgent orders requested for dispatch within 48 hours carry a surcharge of 20 percent of the order value. The urgent surcharge is applied after the volume discount.

4. Delivery
Prices are quoted DAP Mumbai. Delivery to the vessel at any Indian port is free for orders of USD 5,000 or more; below that a flat delivery charge of USD 250 applies.

5. Documentation
Each consignment includes a packing list, invoice and certificate of origin. Maker's certificates are included at no cost for OEM parts. Harbourline also supplies approved non OEM equivalents, which are marked as such on the invoice.

6. Warranty
OEM parts carry a 12 month warranty from delivery. Approved non OEM equivalents carry a 6 month warranty from delivery.

7. Returns
Unused parts in original packing may be returned within 14 days of delivery. A restocking fee of 15 percent of the part value applies.

8. Quotation Validity
Quotations are valid for 21 days from the date of issue.
