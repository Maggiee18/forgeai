# Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy
Corvane Analytics Pvt Ltd | Document IT-POL-01 | Edition 2026
1. Purpose and Scope
This policy applies to every employee, intern and contractor of Corvane Analytics who uses a company laptop, phone, account or network, at the Bengaluru and Pune offices or remotely. Questions about this policy go to the Information Security team at security@corvane.example.
2. Passwords and Multi Factor Authentication
Company account passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any of the last 5 passwords. Passwords must be changed every 90 days; the system prompts you 7 days before expiry. Multi factor authentication through the Corvane Authenticator app is mandatory for email, VPN and every cloud application. Never share a one time code with anyone, including the Service Desk.
3. Locking and Device Care
Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lock your screen manually whenever you leave your desk. Company laptops must not be left in a parked vehicle. Full disk encryption is enabled on every laptop and must not be disabled.
4. Lost or Stolen Devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the Service Desk within 2 hours of discovery, by calling extension 4357 or the after hours line. The Service Desk will remotely lock and wipe the device. A police report is required for stolen devices before a replacement is issued.
5. Removable Media and Personal Devices
USB storage devices are blocked on all company laptops. Exceptions need written approval from the Information Security Manager and are valid for a maximum of 30 days. Personal phones may access email and Teams only after enrolment in the company device management app, which creates a separate work profile. Personal laptops may not connect to the office network.
6. Remote Work and Networks
Off the office network, all access to internal systems must go through the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected. Employees working abroad for more than 14 days must inform Information Security in advance.
7. Data Classification
Corvane data has four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Client datasets are Restricted by default. Restricted data may be stored only in approved client workspaces and must never be copied to email, personal storage or AI tools that are not on the approved software list. Confidential data may be shared externally only through the secure file transfer service.
8. Email and Phishing
Report suspicious emails with the Report Phishing button in Outlook. If you clicked a link or entered credentials, call the Service Desk immediately; this is treated as a Priority 1 security incident. Auto forwarding of company email to external addresses is blocked.
9. Software Installation
Software may be installed only from the Company Portal. Anything not in the Company Portal must be requested under the Hardware and Software Request Procedure (IT-PRC-03) and pass a security review.
10. Security Incidents
Any suspected security incident, including malware alerts, unexpected access requests or data sent to the wrong person, must be reported to security@corvane.example or the Service Desk within 30 minutes of discovery.
11. Monitoring and Violations
Company devices and accounts are monitored for security purposes. Violations of this policy may lead to account suspension and disciplinary action under the HR policy. A first minor violation results in a mandatory security refresher course within 14 days.
