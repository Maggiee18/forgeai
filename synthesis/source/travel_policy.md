# Travel and Expense Policy: Shore Staff
Meridian Shipmanagement Pvt Ltd | Document HR-POL-05 | Edition 2026

1. Scope
This policy covers official travel by shore staff, including vessel attendance, port visits, client meetings, training and audits. Every official trip must be approved in the HRMS travel module before tickets are booked.

2. Employee Grades
Shore staff are grouped into five grades. G1 covers executives and officers, G2 covers assistant managers, G3 covers managers and superintendents, G4 covers general managers, and G5 covers directors.

3. Travel Class Entitlement
For domestic air travel, grades G1 to G3 travel in economy class, and grades G4 and G5 may travel in business class on flights longer than 2 hours. For rail travel, G1 and G2 are entitled to AC 2 tier and G3 to G5 to AC 1st class. All air tickets must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days before travel, except for urgent vessel attendance.

4. Hotel Accommodation
The maximum hotel tariff per night, excluding taxes, is INR 4,500 in Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad) and INR 3,000 in all other cities for grades G1 and G2. For grades G3 and above the caps are INR 7,000 in Tier 1 cities and INR 5,000 in other cities. Hotels must be booked through the travel desk where possible. Staying with friends or relatives is allowed, but no lodging allowance is paid in its place.

5. Daily Allowance
A daily allowance covers meals and incidental expenses. It is INR 1,200 per day for G1 and G2 and INR 1,800 per day for G3 and above. Trips shorter than 8 hours receive half the daily allowance. No daily allowance is paid on days when meals are provided by a client or by the vessel.

6. Local Conveyance
Taxis and app based cabs are reimbursed at actual cost with receipts. Employees who use their own car for official travel are reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre, and own two wheelers at INR 5 per kilometre, based on the distance recorded in the claim.

7. Travel Advance
A travel advance of up to 75 percent of the estimated trip cost may be requested in the HRMS travel module at least 3 working days before travel. Advances must be settled within 15 days of returning. An unsettled advance is recovered from the next salary.

8. Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted in the HRMS portal within 30 days of completing the trip. Claims submitted after 30 days and up to 60 days need approval from the Head of Department. Claims older than 60 days are not paid. Original receipts are required for every item above INR 500.

9. Gifts Received on Official Travel
Gifts received from clients or agents during an official trip may be retained if their value does not exceed INR 5,000. Gifts above INR 5,000 must be handed over to the Admin team on return.

10. Non Reimbursable Expenses
Alcohol, personal entertainment, laundry for trips of under 3 nights, traffic fines, upgrades not covered by entitlement, and expenses of accompanying family members are not reimbursed.

11. International Travel
International travel requires prior approval from a Director. The company pays for visas, travel insurance and a daily allowance of USD 60 per day for all grades. Hotel caps for international travel are set by the travel desk for each city.

12. Combining Official Travel with Leave
An employee may combine an official trip with personal leave if the reporting manager approves. The company pays only the fare that would have applied to the official trip alone. Hotel and daily allowance are not paid for the leave days. Leave days are applied for separately under the Leave Policy.

13. Approvals
Trips are approved by the reporting manager. Trips costing more than INR 1,00,000 in total also need approval from the Head of Department. Claims are verified by the finance team and paid with the next monthly reimbursement cycle.
