# Supplier Commercial Terms: Poseidon Marine Supplies
Approved supplier PMS-017 | Terms valid 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026

1. Payment Terms
Invoices are payable net 45 days from the date of delivery to the vessel or the forwarding agent. Payment within 10 days of delivery earns an early payment discount of 2 percent on the invoice value.

2. Volume Discounts
A volume discount is applied per purchase order on the total order value before freight. Orders of USD 5,000 or more receive a 5 percent discount. Orders of USD 15,000 or more receive an 8 percent discount. Orders of USD 40,000 or more receive a 12 percent discount. Discounts do not combine; only the highest applicable tier applies.

3. Urgent Orders
Urgent orders requested for dispatch within 72 hours carry a surcharge of 15 percent of the order value. The urgent surcharge is applied after the volume discount.

4. Delivery
Prices are quoted FCA Singapore. Delivery to the forwarding agent in Mumbai or Visakhapatnam is free for orders of USD 3,000 or more; below that a flat delivery charge of USD 180 applies. Delivery on board in port is charged at actual launch and agency cost.

5. Documentation
Every consignment ships with a packing list, commercial invoice and maker's certificate of conformity. Class certificates are supplied on request for an additional USD 75 per certificate.

6. Warranty
Genuine OEM parts carry a warranty of 12 months from installation or 18 months from delivery, whichever comes first.

7. Returns
Unused parts in original packing may be returned within 30 days of delivery. A restocking fee of 10 percent of the part value applies. Parts specially ordered from the maker cannot be returned.

8. Quotation Validity
Quotations are valid for 30 days from the date of issue.
