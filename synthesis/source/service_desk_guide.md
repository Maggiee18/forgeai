# IT Service Desk Guide
Corvane Analytics Pvt Ltd | Document IT-GDE-02 | Edition 2026
1. How to Contact the Service Desk
Raise a ticket on the IT Self Service Portal, call extension 4357 (HELP), or email servicedesk@corvane.example. Email requests are converted into portal tickets automatically. The Service Desk is staffed from 8:00 to 20:00 IST, Monday to Saturday. Outside these hours, an after hours line handles Priority 1 incidents only.
2. Ticket Priorities
Priority is set by the Service Desk from impact and urgency. Priority 1 (Critical): a security incident, or an outage stopping a whole team or a client delivery. Priority 2 (High): one user fully unable to work with no workaround. Priority 3 (Medium): a fault with a workaround available. Priority 4 (Low): requests for information, minor issues and cosmetic faults.
3. Response and Resolution Targets
Priority 1: response within 15 minutes, resolution target 4 hours, worked around the clock. Priority 2: response within 30 minutes, resolution target 8 business hours. Priority 3: response within 4 business hours, resolution target 3 business days. Priority 4: response within 1 business day, resolution target 10 business days. Business hours are the Service Desk hours in section 1.
4. Escalation
If a target is breached, the ticket escalates automatically to the Service Desk Lead. If it is still unresolved after a further 50 percent of the resolution target, it escalates to the IT Operations Manager. Employees may also ask for escalation by replying to the ticket with the word ESCALATE.
5. Password Resets and Account Unlocks
Use self service password reset at the login screen; it needs the Corvane Authenticator app. If self service fails, call extension 4357; the agent will verify your identity with your employee ID and a callback to your registered mobile number. Accounts lock after 10 failed sign in attempts and unlock automatically after 30 minutes.
6. Ticket Updates and Closure
You will receive an email at every status change. A ticket marked Resolved closes automatically if you do not reply within 3 business days. A closed ticket can be reopened within 10 business days by replying to the closure email.
7. Major Incidents
For outages affecting more than 25 users, the Service Desk declares a major incident, posts updates on the IT Status page every 30 minutes and sends a closure note with the root cause within 5 business days.
8. Feedback
Each closed ticket sends a one question satisfaction survey. Complaints about the service may be raised with the IT Operations Manager at itops@corvane.example.
