# Hardware and Software Request Procedure
Corvane Analytics Pvt Ltd | Document IT-PRC-03 | Edition 2026
1. Scope
This procedure covers requests for laptops, monitors, accessories, phones and software for Corvane employees. Item prices, lead times and eligible roles are listed in the IT device catalogue (device_catalogue.csv). Items not in the catalogue need a business case.
2. Standard Laptop Entitlement
Every employee receives one standard laptop. Data scientists and engineers are eligible for the performance laptop. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years; an early refresh needs a Service Desk fault report showing the device cannot be repaired economically.
3. How to Request
Submit requests through the IT Self Service Portal under Request Hardware or Request Software. Include the catalogue item code, quantity and business reason. The quoted lead time starts when the last approval is given.
4. Approvals
Requests with a total value below INR 25,000 need approval from the reporting manager only. Requests from INR 25,000 up to INR 1,00,000 also need the IT Operations Manager. Requests above INR 1,00,000 also need Finance approval. Approvers have 2 business days to act before the request is escalated to their own manager.
5. Software Requests
Software in the Company Portal is pre approved and can be installed at once. New software not in the Company Portal needs a security review by Information Security, which takes up to 5 business days, and a licence cost approval under section 4. Free or open source tools also need the security review. Licences unused for 60 days are reclaimed automatically.
6. New Joiners
Hiring managers must request the new joiner kit at least 7 working days before the joining date. The kit is a standard laptop, a headset and accounts. Kits requested later are not guaranteed for day one.
7. Accessories and Monitors
Each employee may have up to 2 external monitors. Accessories under INR 3,000, such as a mouse or keyboard, can be collected from the IT counter at either office without a portal request. Damaged accessories are replaced free once per year; after that the cost is charged to the team budget.
8. Returns and Exit
On exit, all company devices and accessories must be returned to the IT counter by the last working day. Unreturned items are recovered at the catalogue price from the full and final settlement.
9. New Joiner Security Note
On their first day, new joiners set their password and enrol in the Corvane Authenticator app. Passwords must then be changed every 60 days.
