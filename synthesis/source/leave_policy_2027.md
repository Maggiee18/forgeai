# Leave Policy: Shore Staff
Meridian Shipmanagement Pvt Ltd | Document HR-POL-03 | Edition 2027

1. Purpose and Eligibility
This policy sets out the leave entitlements of shore based employees working at the Visakhapatnam head office and the Mumbai branch office. It does not apply to seafarers on articles, whose leave is governed by their Collective Bargaining Agreement. Employees on probation are eligible for sick leave and casual leave from the date of joining, and for annual leave after completing 3 months of service.

2. Leave Year
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Entitlements for employees who join during the year are calculated pro rata from the month of joining.

3. Annual Leave
Every confirmed employee is entitled to 24 days of annual leave per leave year. Annual leave accrues at 2 days for each completed month of service. Annual leave must be applied for at least 7 calendar days in advance, except in an emergency approved by the reporting manager. A single spell of annual leave may not exceed 15 working days without approval from the Head of Department.

4. Carry Forward and Encashment
Unused annual leave of up to 15 days may be carried forward into the next leave year. Any balance above 15 days lapses on 31 December. Employees may encash up to 5 days of unused annual leave once a year, in March, at the basic salary rate. Leave balances are encashed in full at the time of separation, subject to the cap in section 13.

5. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per leave year. Sick leave does not carry forward. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days must be supported by a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, uploaded to the HRMS portal within 3 working days of returning to work. Sick leave may be taken in half days.

6. Casual Leave
Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per leave year. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave and cannot exceed 2 consecutive days. Unused casual leave lapses at the end of the leave year.

7. Maternity Leave
Female employees with at least 80 days of service in the 12 months before the expected date of delivery are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children, of which up to 8 weeks may be taken before delivery. For the third child onwards the entitlement is 12 weeks. Adoptive and commissioning mothers are entitled to 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over.

8. Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

9. Bereavement and Marriage Leave
Employees may take 3 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent in law). Employees may take 3 days of paid marriage leave once during their employment with the company.

10. Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays each calendar year. The holiday list for each office is published by HR in December for the following year. An employee required to work on a public holiday receives a compensatory day off, which must be taken within 30 days.

11. Applying for Leave
All leave is applied for and approved through the HRMS portal. The reporting manager approves annual, casual and sick leave. Maternity, paternity and leave without pay require approval from the Head of HR. Leave that is not applied for in the HRMS portal is treated as unauthorised absence.

12. Leave Without Pay
Leave without pay of up to 30 days may be granted after all paid leave is exhausted, with the approval of the Head of HR. Leave without pay does not count towards service for the purpose of annual leave accrual.

13. Leave During Notice Period
Annual leave cannot be taken during the notice period except with the written approval of the Head of Department. At separation, a maximum of 30 days of accumulated annual leave is encashed.

14. Travel During Leave
Travel undertaken while on leave is personal and is not reimbursed. Where an official trip is combined with personal leave, the rules in the Travel and Expense Policy apply.
