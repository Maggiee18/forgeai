# Spares Procurement Procedure
Meridian Shipmanagement Pvt Ltd | Technical Department | Document TD-PRO-11 | Edition 2026

1. Purpose
This procedure governs how spare parts for the managed fleet are requisitioned, approved, ordered, delivered and received. It applies to all vessels and to the technical department ashore.

2. Raising a Requisition
Requisitions are raised by the Chief Engineer (engine spares) or the Chief Officer (deck and safety spares) in the planned maintenance system. Each requisition line must state the part number, maker, equipment, quantity, and the maintenance job or defect it is needed for. Requisitions for routine spares should be raised at least 60 days before they are needed.

3. Quotations
For orders up to USD 2,000 a single quotation from an approved supplier is sufficient. Orders above USD 2,000 require at least two quotations. Orders above USD 10,000 require three quotations, unless the part is available only from the original equipment maker, in which case the Technical Superintendent records the reason for a single source.

4. Approval Limits
Approval limits apply to the total value of the purchase order after discounts.
Up to USD 2,000: Chief Engineer for engine spares, or Master for deck and safety spares.
Above USD 2,000 and up to USD 10,000: Technical Superintendent.
Above USD 10,000 and up to USD 50,000: Fleet Manager.
Above USD 50,000: Technical Director.
Orders may not be split into smaller orders to avoid an approval limit.

5. OEM and Non OEM Parts
Genuine OEM parts are mandatory for the main engine, auxiliary engines, steering gear and all safety equipment. Approved non OEM equivalents may be used for pumps, compressors, purifiers and deck machinery if the Technical Superintendent approves and the saving is at least 15 percent of the OEM price.

6. Critical Spares
Critical spares are listed in the vessel's critical spares list and must never fall below the minimum quantity on board. The minimum on board for main engine fuel injection valves is 2 complete valves, and for exhaust valve spindles is 1. When stock of a critical spare falls to its minimum, a requisition must be raised within 24 hours.

7. Urgent Requisitions
A requisition is urgent when the equipment is out of service or a class or port state requirement cannot be met. Urgent requisitions are approved by phone or email by the Technical Superintendent and recorded in the planned maintenance system within 1 working day. Urgent surcharges from suppliers are acceptable only for urgent requisitions.

8. Delivery and Receipt
Spares are delivered to the vessel through the forwarding agent at the next suitable port. The Chief Engineer or Chief Officer must confirm receipt in the planned maintenance system within 2 days of delivery, noting any damage or shortage. Damaged items must be photographed and reported to the supplier within 7 days.

9. Records
Purchase orders, quotations, delivery notes and certificates are kept in the document management system for 5 years.
